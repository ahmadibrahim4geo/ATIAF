Analytical channels remain continuous Float32 GeoTIFFs. Quiet/Balanced/Enhanced/Custom display settings affect only separate optional UInt8 display products and previews. Limits come from a documented deterministic sample or explicit fixed bounds; gamma/saturation/settings and exact channels are recorded. Each run exports a reusable preset; imported declarations cannot promote a custom formula to source-verified. QGIS supplies a background before/after preview dialog; Pro supplies temporary before/after HTML through its preview-only option. Preview-only writes no full analytical raster or scene Work/Results. Native map loading is opt-in/queued; RGB display uses explicit QGIS channels and a saved/reopened Pro CIM RGB colorizer with no extra stretch.

The workflow preserves source-first discovery, sensor-first/output-last controls, default/custom scene folders, polygon AOI, projected metre CRS, immutable full/clip outputs, per-run bilingual reports and scientific provenance. Mosaic is same sensor/level/day with a common output CRS; scene priority selects one complete valid RGB triplet, with Source_Scene_ID raster/table, never channel-wise mixing or blind temporal averaging. Repeated runs recompute with unique paths; no cache-hit claim. Fingerprints include analytical parameters/calibration, metadata/QA and source stat identity; they are not full-content source hashes.

## Real user scene and independent results

The later user-provided `Testing Data/LC81760392014234LGN00` superseded the initial no-data reply. It is **pre-Collection Landsat 8 L1T**, 2014-08-22, not surface reflectance. Exact MTL coefficients and solar elevation produce TOA reflectance; no atmospheric correction, dark subtraction or FLAASH is applied. The separate legacy BQA decoder follows USGS Guide v1.4 section 2.1, not Collection 1/2 bit positions. It masks fill/dropped frame/terrain occlusion and medium/high snow/cirrus/cloud confidence; this product has no cloud-shadow or per-band saturation flag. Raw optical 0/65535 are excluded. These limitations are reported.

Actual QGIS English and **actual Pro Arabic (`atiaf_ar`)** ran both full methods. Outputs are **7711 × 7861, 30 m, EPSG:32636, three channels**. The [QGIS receipt](evidence/PLAN-0018/real_qgis_en.json) independently compares **324 valid sample centers per output** against a separate Float64 MTL/solar/formula calculation: maximum absolute errors approximately 5.0e-8 direct / 9.6e-7 ratio. Source sizes/mtime stayed unchanged; metadata hash is recorded.

The Pro helper produced its rasters but failed when formatting its final receipt because ArcPy execution polluted a generic `datetime` global. That helper run is **not called successful**. Its [execution log](evidence/PLAN-0018/real_pro_ar_final.txt), [separate independent artifact readback](evidence/PLAN-0018/pro_real_readback.json) and [whole-raster cross-host comparison](evidence/PLAN-0018/cross_host_equivalence.json) establish the bounded result: **60,616,171 pixels per channel for each method**, identical grids/masks, and **bitwise identical analytical values** between QGIS English and Pro Arabic. This is numerical/product proof, not geological field accuracy or full GUI acceptance. All test outputs are separate under `Testing Results/PLAN-0018/`; supplied scene assets and original plan remain local and unchanged.
