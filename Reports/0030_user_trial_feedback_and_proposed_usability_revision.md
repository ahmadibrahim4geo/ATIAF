## Proposed implementation scope

R2-01…06 in the plan specify actual same-session Pro runtime proof, corrected message attribution/legacy eligibility, short `[study_]index_acquisition-date.tif` names, optional study label in seven tools/EN+AR/both hosts, internal vegetation-index compute/save with NDVI first, and conditional advanced thermal fields with scientific gates. Preserve Results/category/index containers; source scene identity/TOA-vs-SR/formulas/run IDs move to metadata/layer properties instead of long basenames. Changed runs get short collision disambiguation, never overwrite. AOI remains independent; output folder stays last.

No blanket extension of T05 mineral formulas to raw/TOA imagery proposed without per-method review. For official ST, vegetation outputs may accompany it but must not silently correct the official temperature again. For L1, thermal bands and the chosen model's atmospheric/emissivity requirements remain real inputs, not optional physics. Sobrino2004 is TM5-specific; do not transfer its constants uncritically to L8 or another vegetation index.

Primary references read: [Esri environment/package management](https://doc.esri.com/en/arcgis-pro/latest/arcpy/get-started/work-with-python-environments.html), [Esri cloned environments](https://doc.esri.com/en/arcgis-pro/latest/arcpy/get-started/clone-an-environment.html), [USGS official ST/auxiliary inputs](https://www.usgs.gov/landsat-missions/landsat-collection-2-atmospheric-auxiliary-data), [Sobrino2004 original paper](https://www.uv.es/sobrino/sobrino_files/papers/Sobrino_LST-Landsat_RSE2004.pdf). These support environmental isolation and NDVI-emissivity/thermal dependencies; they do not certify a new implementation. Inference: arbitrary vegetation-index substitution would change the model and require new evidence. No source/data vendored, MIT retained.
